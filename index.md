# Deepak Siyal

**Robotics Test and Embedded Engineer** | Pune, India

## About

I build and validate robotic systems on real hardware. Over ten years I have worked across embedded systems, test automation and robotics, from aerospace test stations at Honeywell to humanoids, mobile robots and manipulators at NVIDIA. My focus is on turning complex robotics stacks into reliable, automated, repeatable tests.

*Oct 2020 – Sept 2026*
